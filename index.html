02 / About

Designer by training,
motion designer by obsession.

CDMX, MX

I've spent the last six years making graphics that have to survive live television, arena-scale projection, and brand scrutiny — sometimes all in the same week.

I started in sports media at Fútbol Sites / Tikitaka, where I cut my teeth producing motion content for accounts with 14M+ followers across Latin America. That's where I learned how to work fast, iterate under feedback, and keep brand consistency across dozens of deliverables.

Then I moved into broadcast at Mediapro, designing on-air graphics for Fox Sports and beIN Sports USA. Show days there look like this: 14 videowall loops, 10 full-screens, 1–2 revision cycles, and a hard deadline before airtime. That discipline changed how I build everything — now I think in systems, not assets.

In parallel I freelance on live entertainment — concert visuals for Los Bukis and Marco Antonio Solís World Tours, procedural 3D for Cinépolis theater sequences with Supernova, and VFX support for commercial work with Javich & Co.

Currently based in Mexico City, working remote for teams in the US and Europe. Open to full-time, contract, and freelance.
