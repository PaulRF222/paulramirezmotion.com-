## Optional

- `og-image.jpg` (1200 × 630 px) — preview image when someone shares your site on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X. Make it look like a promo — your name + tagline over a strong still from your work.
- `favicon.svg` or `favicon.ico` — tab icon. Right now the site uses a simple dot; you can upgrade this anytime.

## Image tips

- **Format:** JPG for photos/frames (smaller file size). PNG only if you need transparency.
- **Compression:** Run through tinypng.com or squoosh.app before uploading — target under 300KB per image for fast loading.
- **Colors:** Since the site is dark mode, images with deep blacks/shadows blend beautifully. Very bright white backgrounds may clash — consider cropping out white space.
